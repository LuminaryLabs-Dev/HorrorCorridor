# Goal

## Requirements

- Preserve V1 and implement V2 as its standalone sibling.
- Keep five natural domains authoritative and independently serializable.
- Compose every NexusEngine core explicitly and disable hidden core installation.
- Deliver the solo expedition, Monster Index, offering/building progression, caught/restart, persistence, shared authority, and reconnect loops.
- Provide semantic external control, call timing, deterministic proof, reused live-player proof, and human-view lighting evidence.

## Final Output

The player follows a readable service corridor, locates a stalker by sound, chooses an early study or a full-scare collection, claims the building's offering, and continues deeper. Darkness is survivable while moving; stopping and missing the flashlight response leads to capture. The Index persists across restart and save/load, and shared clients recover the host's authoritative expedition.

Status: in progress. The continuous-corridor slice is live-proven; remaining gameplay features still require player acceptance one UX feature at a time.
